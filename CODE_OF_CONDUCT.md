# Code of Conduct

Stark's community spaces are for constructive technical collaboration. Treat
other participants with respect, assume good faith while examining evidence,
and critique ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized
language, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing another
person's private information, and retaliation against someone who reports a
problem.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe, productive project.
Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, repetition, impact,
and whether the participant takes responsibility and repairs harm.

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's private repository reporting
or security-advisory channel. If that channel is unavailable, open a public
issue requesting private maintainer contact without identifying the people or
details involved. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release coordination, and other spaces where someone represents the Stark
project.
